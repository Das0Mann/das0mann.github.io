  <span class="news-meta">9 September 2026 · BfS research report</span>
  <h3>Research report on the radical-pair mechanism and radiation protection</h3>
  <p>The German Federal Office for Radiation Protection (BfS) published the research report <em>Ist der Radikalpaar-Mechanismus für den Strahlenschutz relevant?</em> by Ilia A. Solov’yov and Luca Gerhards as BfS-RESFOR-269/26. The project examines whether weak magnetic fields can affect radical reactions in biological model systems including cryptochrome, lipid peroxides and NADPH oxidase. <a href="https://doris.bfs.de/jspui/handle/urn%3Anbn%3Ade%3A0221-2026090862461" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BfS report →</a></p>
