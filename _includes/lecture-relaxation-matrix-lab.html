Interactive Liouville-space operator · one spin-½

How rates populate the relaxation matrix

Start with the smallest meaningful open spin system. Compare downward relaxation (|↑⟩ → |↓⟩), upward excitation and pure dephasing. The generator acts on four density-matrix elements, not four physical spin levels.

Relaxation + coherent generator, 4 × 4 (µs⁻¹)

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal L\rho&=-i[\omega S_z,\rho]+\gamma_\downarrow\mathcal D[\sigma_-]\rho +\gamma_\uparrow\mathcal D[\sigma_+]\rho +\frac{\gamma_\varphi}{2}\mathcal D[\sigma_z]\rho,\\ \mathcal D[L]\rho&=L\rho L^\dagger-\tfrac12\{L^\dagger L,\rho\}. \end{aligned} \]

Conventions: |↑⟩ is the first basis state, |↓⟩ the second; σ₋=|↓⟩⟨↑|. The density vector is (ρ↑↑,ρ↑↓,ρ↓↑,ρ↓↓). Rates are in µs⁻¹; ω is an angular frequency in rad/µs. γφ is the additional decay of the off-diagonal density element, not the coefficient in front of D[σz]. The stationary state is unique only when γ↓+γ↑ > 0.
