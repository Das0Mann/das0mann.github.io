Which particles contribute to the electronic Hamiltonian?

The nuclei create an attractive potential for an electron. Move the nuclei apart or switch between H, H₂⁺ and H₂; then relate every interaction in the drawing to a term in the Hamiltonian above.

In H₂⁺, one electron feels attraction to both nuclei. The nuclei repel one another, but there is no electron–electron term.

Model boundary: the plotted curve is a softened one-dimensional Coulomb slice (not a computed orbital, probability density or molecular potential-energy surface). The particle drawing is illustrative, not a classical electron trajectory.
