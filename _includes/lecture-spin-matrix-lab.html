From physical interactions to matrix elements

Choose a pair of spins and change its interactions. The colored entries show which product-basis states are coupled. A non-zero off-diagonal element transfers amplitude between those basis states; a diagonal term changes their relative phases.

Matrix elements, in angular-frequency units (rad/µs)

Dimensionless spin operators use Sᵢ=σᵢ/2. The displayed matrix is H/ℏ, so its entries are angular frequencies, not energy in joules. The Liouvillian is the coherent generator −i[H/ℏ,ρ], with row-major vectorization |a⟩⟨b|. Its 16×16 representation acts on density matrices, not on 16 spin states.
